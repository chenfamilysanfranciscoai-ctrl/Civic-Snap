How it works

From "I should help" to signed up, in minutes

No more hunting through calendars and newsletters. Civic Snap does the gathering, and the organizer handles the sign-up.

We gather the events

Listings are collected automatically from each group's own public calendar and refreshed regularly, so what you see is current.

You find what fits

Search by what you want to do, how active you'd like to be, where, and when. Every event carries plain-language tags.

You sign up with the organizer

Each event links straight to the organizer's own sign-up page, so you always register with the group you'll be helping.
